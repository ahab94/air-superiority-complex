# Air Superiority Complex v0.1.0

The first release: a satirical top-down arcade shooter in real-time 3D, with parody jets, made-up countries and ten missions loosely inspired by real Cold War and border-war incidents.

**Play in your browser:** https://ahab94.github.io/air-superiority-complex/

## What's in it
- **10 missions in 2 chapters.** Each has its own terrain and weather, and a rule that comes from its setting:
  - an unarmed spy-plane run past missile sites;
  - a sanctuary river you mustn't shoot across;
  - an afterburner dash that overheats;
  - icing on the wings, and a four-prop boss bomber;
  - ground flak, monsoon gusts and night fighting;
  - mountain turbulence;
  - a missiles-only duel across "the Line".
- **Dueling Ministries of Claims.** Chapter 2 counts *Kills vs Claimed*, and both sides' press offices issue their own figures.
- **Cannons, armor and lock-on missiles.** Every jet takes several hits and visibly struggles. Hold to lock 1–4 targets, release to fire. Unlimited flares on a recharge decoy enemy missiles.
- **13 parody jets** with different speed, agility, armor, guns and missiles.
- **Optional local co-op.** A second player drops in with Enter or gamepad 2 at any time.
- **Touch controls on phones and tablets** (stick, Fire, Missile, Flare, Pause).
- **All music and sound synthesized from scratch:** a lounge loop for the bar and two battle themes.

## Install
- **Browser:** the link above.
- **macOS / Linux:** `curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ahab94/air-superiority-complex/main/install.sh | bash`
- **Windows (PowerShell):** `irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ahab94/air-superiority-complex/main/install.ps1 | iex`
