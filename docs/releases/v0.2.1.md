# Air Superiority Complex v0.2.1

- **A real difficulty curve.** Every mission now gets steadily harder, from a gentle first sortie to the Kill Chain. Enemies fire more often and faster, aim better, carry more armor and launch more missiles as you progress. Chapter finales spike. Tuned by measuring a "rookie" pilot across all 15 missions.
- **Modern enemy jets pop flares** at your missiles, so the long-range duels last longer.
- **Stealth is modern-only and only with an AWACS.** The 1970s phantom ace now fades into mist instead.
- **Fixed:** a shot-down jet could stay frozen mid-crash into the next mission.
