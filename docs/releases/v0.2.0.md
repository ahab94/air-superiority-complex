# Air Superiority Complex v0.2.0: Chapter 3, Greatest Hits

Five new missions, each with a new idea:

- **Operation Bowl-o:** fly disguised as a slow bomber. Enemies close in without firing; hold your nerve, then ambush them for double points.
- **The Ace Who Wasn't:** Colonel Nobody, a phantom ace who blinks in and out of existence. Rumours included.
- **A Line, Allegedly:** rules of engagement. Weapons hold until they fire first. Three violations and you're court-martialled.
- **Sea Harrumph:** attackers climb up from behind. Pull back to air-brake and watch them overshoot into your guns.
- **The Kill Chain:** radar planes cloak their fighters in shimmering stealth camo. Cloaked jets can still shoot you; bring down every Big Ear to unveil them.

Also new:
- **7 parody jets,** including a radar plane with a spinning dish.
- **Two new landscapes:** misty karst hills, and a sea of clouds.
